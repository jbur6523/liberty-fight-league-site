How the lines work

Each fighter starts with 2 baseline votes for percentages only. Every matchup opens at PK (50/50); that opening line never changes. Fan pick counts include real picks only.

Displayed percentages sum to 100 and stay between 1% and 99%. Fan Odds convert those percentages to a moneyline, rounded to the nearest 5. PK means an even split.

Movement is the change in percentage points since the 50% opening, not movement over a recent time window. Biggest Mover and Biggest Favorite can be the same fighter because every line opens evenly.

Most Picked is the fighter with the most real picks in a current matchup. Biggest Favorite has the highest share above 50%; Closest Line is the most evenly split matchup.

One active pick per matchup in this browser. Change your pick while voting is open; it still counts as one. Anonymous polling cannot guarantee one person, one vote.
